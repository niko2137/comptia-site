About Niko

I've spent years working in IT — troubleshooting hardware, managing networks, securing systems, and helping people understand technology that can feel overwhelming at first. The CompTIA A+ certification was one of my early milestones, and I remember exactly what it felt like to study for it: endless books that listed every objective without ever helping you understand it.

That frustration is what drove me to write the CompTIA A+ Complete Study Guide. I wanted a resource that reads like a conversation with a colleague who's already passed — one that tells you what actually matters on the exam, calls out the tricky wording you'll encounter, and gives you the mental models that make concepts stick.

The Members Portal — with the games, flashcards, glossary, and practice tools on this site — is an extension of that same philosophy. Learning should be active, not passive. You remember what you interact with.

About the Book

The CompTIA A+ Complete Study Guide covers every exam objective for both the Core 1 (220-1201) and Core 2 (220-1202) exams. It's structured around how technicians actually think — not around how CompTIA organizes their objective list.

Every topic is priority-tagged so you know what to memorize cold versus what to skim. Exam traps are called out explicitly. Original diagrams and mnemonics give you mental shortcuts that generic books don't provide. And every format — Kindle, Paperback, or Hardback — includes the Members Portal access code on the last page.
