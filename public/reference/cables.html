Ethernet Cables

Category Max Speed Max Distance Notes
Cat 5e 1 Gbps 100m Minimum for Gigabit. Enhanced crosstalk reduction
Cat 6 10 Gbps (55m) / 1 Gbps (100m) 55–100m Plastic spine separator. Thicker than 5e
Cat 6a 10 Gbps 100m Augmented. Full 10G at full distance. Shielded
Cat 8 25–40 Gbps 30m Data centers only. Shielded

Video Connectors

Connector Max Resolution Audio? Notes
VGA 1080p (analog) No 15-pin blue. Legacy. Analog only
DVI-D 1080p–1440p No Digital only. White connector
DVI-I 1080p–1440p No Digital + analog
HDMI 4K–8K Yes 19-pin. Most common. No latch
DisplayPort 4K–8K Yes 20-pin with latch. Daisy-chain support

🔥 Cable Type Notes (Building Code)

  • Plenum (CMP): Fire-retardant for HVAC/ceiling spaces. Required by building code.
  • Riser (CMR): Fire-resistant for vertical runs between floors.
  • PVC: Standard. Only for open areas, NOT plenum/riser spaces.
