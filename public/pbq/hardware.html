Match motherboard components to their descriptions

📋 Instructions

This is a drag-and-drop matching exercise. You need to match each motherboard component to its correct description.

  • Desktop: Drag a component name from the bank on the right and drop it onto the correct slot.
  • Mobile/Touch: Tap a component in the bank to select it (it will turn green), then tap a drop zone to place it.
  • To remove a placed item, click/tap on it in the drop zone.
  • Click "Check Answers" when you've placed all items. You need 80% (10/12) to pass.
