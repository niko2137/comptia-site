## ENTRY #1: USB PD 3.1 / EPR

### Copy this text exactly:

**USB PD 3.1 / EPR** — USB Power Delivery 3.1 with Extended Power Range. Advanced USB-C charging specification supporting up to 240W of power delivery (48V @ 5A) for high-performance devices like gaming laptops and workstations. Introduces three new voltage levels: 28V (140W), 36V (180W), and 48V (240W). Requires EPR-rated cables specifically marked for USB PD 3.1 to achieve full wattage. Backward compatible with USB PD 2.0/3.0 (100W max) through automatic negotiation between device, charger, and cable capabilities.

---

## ENTRY #2: Wi-Fi 6E

### Copy this text exactly:

**Wi-Fi 6E** — Extension of Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) that operates in the newly available 6 GHz frequency band, providing 1200 MHz of additional spectrum, 59 non-overlapping 20 MHz channels, and significantly reduced interference compared to the crowded 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands. Supports up to seven clean 160 MHz channels for consistent multi-gigabit speeds. Ideal for high-density environments, AR/VR applications, and 8K streaming. Requires both Wi-Fi 6E-capable router and client devices. Not backward compatible with Wi-Fi 6-only devices on the 6 GHz band—legacy devices connect on 2.4/5 GHz instead.
