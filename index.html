About the Author

Niko Williams is a CompTIA A+ certified IT professional, entrepreneur, and technical writer with a singular mission: making complex technology accessible to everyone.

Niko's journey into tech began in the late 1990s — an era of dial-up and CRT monitors. Since then, Niko has built a diverse career spanning IT support, hardware repair, web and graphic design, consulting, and technical documentation. This guide is the product of that decades-long journey — built for people who need to pass without wasting time on content that won't be tested.
